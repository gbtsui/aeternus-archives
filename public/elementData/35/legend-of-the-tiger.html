Legend of the Tiger
Liu Jinyu / 劉金玉
In the years of old
When the vernal winds blew around Mount Xiangshan
In the ages of ancient
When the spring warmth melted the ice of the Two Rivers
A tiger of steel ripped through the peace
The flames of war stopped him not
As he galloped on forwards
Among the autumn leaves in his path
Through the mist and the smoke
Through the blood-soaked ground
He quenched the blaze of wrath
He pacified the war-torn land
The General of Vehement Might
Stood steadfast, unwavering
A knife to the back of those who wronged him
And victory in the duels of honor
He knew not defeat
Nor was his pride shaken by death
His valiance had no bounds
And his strength unfettered by border
Mist blows from the Eastern islands
The phantom comes to hunt him
Undeterred, he presses on
And the blades of East and West clash
The tiger hunts no more
Lost in the fog
The summer breeze flows around the mountains
The water freezes once again
